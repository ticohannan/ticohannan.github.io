# Inbox

Add one line per item you'd like on the site or profile. The next scheduled review folds these in
and removes them. A date and a link help.

Examples of the format (delete these once you add real items):
- 2026-11 · Presented a poster at <conference> on <topic> · <link>
- 2026-12 · Finished <course> project: <one line> · <repo or video link>
